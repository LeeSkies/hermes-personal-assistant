Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Scope

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public or to third parties.

Google data accessed

The application requests access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It may read, search, organize, draft, and send email, and read or manage calendar events, only to perform tasks requested by the account owner.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide personal-assistant functions requested by the account owner. Relevant content may be sent to the language-model provider configured by the owner when necessary to perform a requested task. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by the application operator to train generalized machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and application state are stored on the owner's self-hosted server. Requested task results and conversation history may also be stored locally so sessions can be resumed. Data is retained until the owner deletes it or revokes access.

Sharing

Google user data is not disclosed to unrelated third parties. It is processed only by services selected by the owner and only as needed to perform requested functions, comply with law, or protect the application and account.

Security

OAuth tokens are stored with restricted filesystem permissions. Access to the assistant is restricted to the owner's approved accounts. No Google password is collected or stored.

Revocation and deletion

The owner may revoke Google access at any time from Google Account permissions. The owner may delete locally stored OAuth tokens, session history, and cached data from the self-hosted server at any time.

Contact

Questions may be submitted through the project issue tracker.
