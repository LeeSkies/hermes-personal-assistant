Hermes Personal Assistant

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted assistant operated solely for its owner's personal use.

Google integration

The application connects to the owner's Google account through OAuth to:

  • Read, search, organize, draft, and send Gmail messages at the owner's direction.
  • Read and manage Google Calendar events at the owner's direction.

This is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
